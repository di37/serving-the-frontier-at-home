What Comes Next

A living document, rebuilt from source on every push. Two revisions are planned: four Sparks — Chapter 17 ships a TP=4 launcher that its own header calls untested, and it stays that way until a four-node kit exists to measure it — and heavier models, which change the weights-versus-KV split every capacity figure here rests on. Until then the honest scope of this edition is two nodes, and the book says so wherever it looks past that line.

Written about the open engineering of Mia's AI Lab (@MiaAI_lab), who publish their patches, benchmarks, changelogs and retractions in full. This book would not be possible otherwise.
