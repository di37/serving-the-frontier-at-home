Head/worker asymmetry. The worker's port facing the head is often a differently named device than the head's port facing the worker, so one shared `.env.dspark` cannot describe both nodes. Two plausible mechanisms are ruled out — a shared or `scp`'d env file and Compose variable substitution both feed the worker values named for the head — and the launcher instead injects the `WORKER_*` family as process environment into every remote `docker compose` call, rebuilding it per call so a GID resolve after early init is still visible. Each `WORKER_*` defaults to its head counterpart, and `remote_nccl_env2` extends the pattern to the optional TP=3 lane.
