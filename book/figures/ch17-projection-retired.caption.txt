The elegant method, and the measurement that retired it. Classic abliteration orthogonalizes each `o_proj` weight against a published refusal direction *r*, so that `rᵀW' = (1 − α) rᵀW`: at α=1 the refusal component is removed exactly, and at the published α=3.0 it is inverted and doubled, while components orthogonal to *r* are untouched. The overlay's tests verify all of that to unusual depth — including that editing the full 4096 × 8192 weight and editing two 4096 × 4096 column shards independently agree to under 10⁻², which is what makes a per-rank load-time edit legal. What the lab then measured is the rest of the story: the shipped directions' maximum |rᵀW| against stock `o_proj` was 0.084, exactly random-unit-vector level. The publisher's own projection variants top out at 9/32 on their refusal-bypass gate; byte-copying the donor's weights scores 32/32.
