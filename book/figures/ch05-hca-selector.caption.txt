The `NCCL_IB_HCA` selector is a grammar applied to a universe NCCL has already built. Top: token anatomy — optional `^` exclusion, optional `=` exact match, comma-separated `name[:port[:rail[:plane]]]` — with `atoi` port semantics, where `devA` and `devA:` are both the −1 any-port wildcard, `:08` is decimal 8 and `:abc` is port 0, and the nine-digit clamp that stops `18446744073709551615` from wrapping to the wildcard. Bottom: selection is two filters in series. `ncclIbInit` keeps only ACTIVE Ethernet or InfiniBand ports, capped at 32, before the selector is consulted, so a DOWN sibling port can never fail a match — and a validator that models only the selector checks devices NCCL was never going to open.
