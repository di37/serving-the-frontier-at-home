The four states of a two-source cross-check. The sizing formula can only read `hf_text_config.index_kpool`, while the authoritative `kv_cache_spec.compress_ratio` resolves later in the metadata builder's `__init__` — "same number by construction, but construction is not a check." The PR-8 draft guarded the comparison behind `self.compress_ratio > 1`, which reads as a harmless optimisation and in fact shades out the entire right-hand column, including the one cell that under-sizes: config says pools, runtime hands raw token counts, and a worst case of 16 × 1,000,007 ≈ 16M entries meets a workspace sized for 4,000,032 — over 3.9× under. The shipped guard raises unconditionally in both directions, on every rank at every boot, and three tests pin it: two behavioural, one on the source form itself, because a behavioural test alone could pass against a merely reordered guard.
