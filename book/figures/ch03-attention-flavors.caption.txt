One bargain, repeated 43 times. Every layer attends the most recent 128 tokens exactly; older context survives only as compressed rows — one per 4 tokens on the 21 C4A layers, one per 128 on the 20 C128A layers — and on C4A layers a query may read only the 512 rows the Lightning indexer selects. Below 2,048 tokens every candidate already fits in the top-512, so the selection provably cannot change the answer.
