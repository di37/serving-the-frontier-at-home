The drafter's only cross-step state. Each draft attention module owns a persistent `main_kv_cache[max_num_seqs, window, head_dim]` — a per-request sliding window of projected target features written as a ring at `position % window` — and draft attention runs over `[main-window | draft-block]` with an attention-sink term in the softmax normalizer. `initialize_attn_backend` is a no-op (`block_size = 1`, no KV layers registered), so this sidesteps the paged-KV allocator entirely. Reads gather and writes scatter through `slot_index`; when the permutation is the identity the proposer passes `slot_index=None` and the original in-place path runs byte-for-byte.
