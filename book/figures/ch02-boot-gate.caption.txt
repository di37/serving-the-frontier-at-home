Boot, in order. The launcher normalizes configuration, validates the fabric, syncs patches over SSH and preflights both ranks, then starts the worker first so rank 1 is waiting before rank 0 initializes its TCPStore. Inside each container the patch train runs before exec vllm and any failure kills the container rather than serving stock bytes; the hazard lane shows the converse, an unsupervised restart policy resurrecting the two ranks independently.
